01 / METHOD

Draft fast, verify once

Speculative decoding trades a cheap drafter's guesses against one parallel verification pass. The dashboard surfaces acceptance-at-position for every drafter, so you can see exactly how deep the drafts hold up.

02 / PIPELINE

Fresh evals, on every merge

Nightly runs re-benchmark every published drafter against its target on fixed hardware and fixed datasets. Regressions show up in the table before they show up in production.
